Senior Middleware & Infrastructure Engineer with 15+ years in enterprise IT, specializing in IBM Sterling Managed File Transfer (File Gateway, B2B Integrator, Connect:Direct), Linux systems administration, and AWS/Azure cloud infrastructure. Over the last five-plus years, hands-on supporting business-critical MFT environments for a Fortune 500 telecommunications provider, in 24x7 and follow-the-sun production models.

At DXC Technology, I lead middleware support for a large-scale Managed File Transfer platform built on IBM Sterling File Gateway, B2B Integrator, and Connect:Direct for UNIX. As team lead, I coordinate priorities, escalations, and incident response, drive root cause analysis for critical incidents and major outages, and work directly with IBM Support on product-level issues. My scope spans partner onboarding, ServiceNow-based change management, disaster recovery, Bash automation, and Power BI reporting for leadership visibility.

Before DXC, I supported the same Sterling/Linux stack at Kyndryl, alongside Azure infrastructure and 24x7 on-call coverage. Earlier, I spent five years at Amazon Web Services in large-scale data center operations, leading a hardware decommissioning team, delivering infrastructure rollout projects, and building internal technical-interview and training programs for new hires. I started my career administering on-premises and AWS infrastructure across multiple corporate locations, leading a small support team.

My goal is to become a recognized expert in IBM Sterling technologies and applied AI, while deepening my cloud architecture skills toward AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Associate. I believe technology should be secure, scalable, reliable, and simple to operate.
